## 1. Bundled Public-Domain Texts

### Holy Bible (`app/src/main/assets/bible_bilingual.db`)
- **King James Version (KJV, 1769):** Public Domain.
- **Bible in Basic English (BBE, 1949/1965):** Public Domain in the United States.
- **Edisana Ŋwed Abasi (Classic Efik Bible, traditional Hugh Goldie / NBSS orthography):** Public Domain historical translation.

### Hymnals (`app/src/main/assets/*.json`)
- **Classic Hymns (`soc_hymns.json`):** 115 pre-1925 public-domain Christian hymns.
- **Ŋwed Ikwọ (`efik_hymns.json`):** Traditional classic Efik church hymnal texts.
- **Spiritual Songs (`new_hymns.json`):** 12 pre-1925 public-domain hymns.
